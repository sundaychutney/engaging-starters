Prints roughly A5-sized cards, two per A4 sheet with a cut line between them — a fresh, unique set every time you print.

Pick a difficulty, then press "Next clue" to read out a clue — students mark the matching element symbol on their printed card. Elements never repeat until you start a new game. Higher difficulty mixes in harder clue types (name, a defining property, a fuller description) rather than replacing the easier ones.
